Test 4 was rewritten on 2026-09-27: its earlier rubric required `icon-text-fit`, which none of OSM Bright's or OSM Liberty's shield layers set; they pick a pre-drawn badge per ref length. Its term check lists three spellings of "zoom function", since the pinned model writes it with a U+2011 hyphen, and the rubric alone passed two baseline runs. Test 1 no longer credits OSM Liberty with OSM Bright's image names; its one `icontains` is `us-interstate_`, since `ref_length` is in the prompt and `us-interstate` matched an invented `road-shield-us-interstate`. Its `not-regex` fails a line that runs a network's badges from `_1` past the last one either sheet carries; the rubric no longer lists those IDs, since the judge reported them in answers that never named them. Probes of the choice itself (a `Marker` collision option, a draggable pin beside 3,000 depots) passed at baseline, so the decision tree stays short.
